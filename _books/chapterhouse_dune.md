This book was seriously difficult to get through. I only finished it because I really wanted to have completely read all of Frank Herbert's Dune series--but it was a struggle to finish due to how poorly it was written and how far Frank Herbert had fallen by the time he wrote Chapterhouse: Dune in terms of his ability to create fascinating fantasy worlds.
